Our Story

Rooted in Odisha's Soil

Jibamrit Agro (OPC) Private Limited was born from a deep belief — that the earth, when treated with respect, gives back abundantly. Founded under the visionary leadership of Sebananda Samal, Jibamrit has been nurturing the fertile lands of Kendrapara, Odisha since 2025.

Incorporated on 14th September 2025 as a One Person Company under the Companies Act 2013, Jibamrit Agro brings together decades of farming expertise with a modern commitment to organic practices. We operate under the umbrella of Greenex Herbal Clinic, a trusted name in the region since 1995.

From wholesale trading in fruits & vegetables, dairy, edible oils, spices, tea and staple grains — everything we do starts with one principle: no chemicals, no shortcuts, just nature's best.

What Drives Us

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To make organic, chemical-free food accessible to every household by connecting farmers directly with consumers — building a healthier India, one harvest at a time.

Our Vision

To become Odisha's most trusted name in organic wholesale — a brand that farmers, retailers and consumers can rely on for purity, quality and fair value.

Our Promise

Every product bearing the Jibamrit name is grown without synthetic pesticides or chemical fertilisers — a promise rooted in our tagline: "Healthy soil, Healthy soul."
